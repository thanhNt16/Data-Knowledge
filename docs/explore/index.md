# 🪐 Knowledge Galaxy

> **Why it matters** — Reading chapters linearly hides how concepts connect. The galaxy lays the whole curriculum out as one 3D graph: every star is a concept, every beam is a dependency or relation, and every color cluster is a track.

<KnowledgeGalaxy />

## How to fly

- **Drag** to orbit · **scroll / pinch** to zoom · **right-drag** (or two-finger) to pan
- **Hover** a star to light up it and its neighborhood — everything else dims
- **Click** a star for its detail card: what it is, what it depends on, the chapter that teaches it, and a quick self-check question
- Stars **pulse by kind** — track cores are the brightest, chapters mid-size, concepts small
- The detail card's chapter link keeps your place; the browser Back button returns you here

## Reading the clusters

Each track forms its own cluster color (legend in the card). Cross-cluster beams are the interesting ones — e.g. *watermarks* bridging Streaming ↔ System Design, *salting* bridging Spark ↔ Intuitions, *data contracts* bridging DataOps ↔ Streaming. If you can explain any cross-cluster beam, you're integrating knowledge across silos — exactly what senior interviews test.

Start with the [🗺️ Knowledge Map](/map/) for a linear path, or dive into any [track](/) from the home page.
