## Why this exists

Most Data Engineering material is either a shallow listicle or a 500-page book. This knowledge base takes the **Pareto path**: the ~20% of concepts, trade-offs, and failure modes that show up in ~80% of real systems and senior-level interviews.

Every chapter is built to be visual first:

- 📊 **Diagrams over prose** — architecture, dataflow, and lifecycle diagrams in every chapter
- 📋 **Comparison tables** — because senior work is choosing between options, not reciting definitions
- ⚠️ **Warnings & risks** — the failure modes you only learn from production incidents
- 💡 **Senior insights** — the "why" behind conventions
- 🧠 **Quizzes** — active recall at the end of every chapter, with explanations
